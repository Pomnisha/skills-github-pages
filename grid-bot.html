How Grid Trading Works

1

Set the Grid

Define a price range and divide it into equal intervals. Each interval is one "grid level."

2

Place Orders

Place buy orders below the current price and sell orders above it at every grid line.

3

Price Drops → Buy

When price falls to a grid line, the bot buys. It immediately places a sell order one level higher.

4

Price Rises → Sell

When price rises to the sell level, the bot sells for a profit equal to one grid interval.

5

Repeat

The bot profits every time price oscillates within the grid — more volatility means more profit.
